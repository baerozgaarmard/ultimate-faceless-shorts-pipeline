# Faceless Shorts — Style Sheets & Visual References

This folder contains the official reference style sheets for all 6 micro-short styles in the **Faceless Shorts** engine. Every style includes camera rules, color palettes, lighting presets, typography, and procedural animation standards.

---

## 1. Deep Dive 3D (Flagship Style)
- **Reference Image**: `deep-dive-style-sheet.png`
- **Vibe**: High-energy, anatomical, medical, and scientific 3D explainers (High-detail 3D anatomical aesthetic).
- **Core Visuals**:
  - Medical 3D cross-sections (skin, teeth, bones, lungs, muscles).
  - Volumetric lighting and ambient occlusion with procedural skin/tissue shaders.
  - Floating kinetic 3D labels with pointer pins.
  - Gliding camera orbits that dive into microscopic details.
- **Best For**: "What happens if you hold your breath?", "What happens if you never sleep?", "Why do knuckles pop?".

---

## 2. Night File 3D (True Crime & Mystery)
- **Reference Image**: `night-file-style-sheet.png`
- **Vibe**: Dark, cinematic, noir, unsolved mysteries, conspiratorial archives, and true crime files.
- **Core Visuals**:
  - Moody 3D rooms lit by a single desk lamp or computer CRT monitor.
  - Manila case folders, red string corkboards, redacted typewriter documents.
  - Realistic shadows, dust motes, and dark vignette depth of field.
  - Slow eerie dolly zooms and security camera perspective cuts.
- **Best For**: "The man who vanished at 30,000 feet (D.B. Cooper)", "The mystery of the Mary Celeste", "Unsolved heist files".

---

## 3. Inside Out (2D X-Ray Reveal)
- **Reference Image**: `inside-out-style-sheet.png`
- **Vibe**: Clean vector x-ray explainers with translucent cutaways.
- **Core Visuals**:
  - High-contrast silhouette characters with illuminated internal biology/mechanics.
  - Neon diagnostic highlights showing object travel paths (e.g. food, water, foreign objects).
  - Minimalist color grading: Deep obsidian backdrops with cyan/emerald/amber highlight glows.
  - Clear, diagrammatic motion graphics.
- **Best For**: "What happens if you swallow chewing gum?", "How does anesthesia knock you out?", "What swallowed batteries do inside you".

---

## 4. Stick Story (Minimalist Survival)
- **Reference Image**: `stick-story-style-sheet.png`
- **Vibe**: Clean hand-drawn stick figures navigating urgent survival, rule, and life situations.
- **Core Visuals**:
  - Expressive stick figures with bold ink outlines and dynamic stick poses.
  - High-impact warning signs, hazard zones, item callouts, and numbered steps.
  - Crisp monochrome or two-tone aesthetic with punchy accent colors (red, amber, safety yellow).
  - Rapid, punchy pacing with humorous stick reactions.
- **Best For**: "3 rules to survive a bear attack", "What if the power went out for 7 days?", "How to escape quicksand".

---

## 5. Paper Atlas (Historical Cartography)
- **Reference Image**: `paper-atlas-style-sheet.png`
- **Vibe**: Textured paper layers, vintage maps, parchment cutouts, and historical reenactment.
- **Core Visuals**:
  - Multi-plane papercraft layers with realistic drop shadows and fibrous parchment textures.
  - Animated antique nautical & topographical maps with inked marching routes, battle lines, and trade routes.
  - Cut-paper figures, ships, and architectural silhouettes moving across miniature paper stages.
  - Warm sepia, terracotta, and olive vintage color palette.
- **Best For**: "How one tiny bakery burned down all of London", "The voyage that mapped the edge of the world", "The lost Roman legion".

---

## 6. Tall Tale 3D (Animated Storytime)
- **Reference Image**: `tall-tale-style-sheet.png`
- **Vibe**: Hilarious, bizarre, true-life historical anecdotes with flat 2D cartoon characters in a vibrant 3D world.
- **Core Visuals**:
  - Flat cartoon bean-head card characters with thick ink outlines.
  - Vibrant, sunny 3D towns, vehicles, and interiors rendered with painterly toon textures.
  - Slapstick camera tracking, squash-and-stretch pop animations, and sound-effect visual bursts.
  - Cheerful saturated skies, soft depth-of-field background blur.
- **Best For**: "The girl who was mailed to grandma via USPS", "The war fought over a wooden bucket", "The great molasses flood".
